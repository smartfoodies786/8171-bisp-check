Taleemi Wazaif Check Online by CNIC 2026

Last verified: 24 Nov 2026

Benazir Taleemi Wazaif is a quarterly education stipend for the children of women who are active BISP Kafaalat beneficiaries. It is paid per child, to help keep children in school. This page explains how to check it, the rates, and what to do if it does not come.

Check Taleemi Wazaif by the Mother's CNIC

Taleemi Wazaif is checked by the mother's CNIC — the same CNIC used for the Kafaalat check — on the official 8171 portal, or by SMS to 8171. If the mother is an active Kafaalat beneficiary and the child is enrolled, the stipend shows with the payment.

Open the Official 8171 Portal

Opens 8171.bisp.gov.pk in a new tab. CNIC number + picture code only — no login, no fee. Or send the mother's 13-digit CNIC by SMS to 8171.

Taleemi Wazaif Rates 2026

The stipend is paid per child, per quarter. The amount depends on the child's education level, and girls get Rs 500 more than boys at each level.

Who Gets Taleemi Wazaif?

  • The mother must be an active Kafaalat beneficiary — if her Kafaalat payment has stopped, the children's stipend stops too.
  • The child must be enrolled in school and registered for Taleemi Wazaif at the BISP Tehsil Office.
  • The child must attend at least 70% of school days. If attendance falls below 70%, the stipend can stop.
  • Age limits apply by education level — confirm the exact age band for your child's level at enrolment.

How to Enrol a Child for Taleemi Wazaif

  1. Check the mother is an active Kafaalat beneficiary on the 8171 portal or by SMS.
  2. Go to the BISP Tehsil Office with the mother's original CNIC and the child's enrolment documents from the school.
  3. Register the child for Taleemi Wazaif. Enrolment is free.
  4. Keep attendance above 70% and keep the school record updated, or the stipend can stop.

Stipend Delayed or Missing — What to Do

  • Check the mother's Kafaalat status first — if she is not active, the children's stipend will not come either.
  • Check the child's attendance — below 70% stops the stipend.
  • Check the payment on the 8171 portal or by SMS to 8171.
  • Still missing? Visit the BISP Tehsil Office with the mother's original CNIC and the child's enrolment record.
  • If money was deducted from the stipend, report it on helpline 0800-26477. Complaint & helpline guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Benazir Taleemi Wazaif?

Taleemi Wazaif is a quarterly education stipend for the children of women who are active BISP Kafaalat beneficiaries. It is paid per child to help keep children in school.

How do I check Taleemi Wazaif online by CNIC?

Check it by the mother's CNIC on the official 8171 portal at 8171.bisp.gov.pk — the same portal as the Kafaalat check — or by SMS to 8171. If the mother is an active Kafaalat beneficiary and the child is enrolled, the stipend shows with the payment.

How much is the Taleemi Wazaif stipend in 2026?

The stipend is Rs 2,500 to Rs 5,000 per child per quarter, depending on the education level, with girls getting Rs 500 more than boys at each level. A Rs 500 raise was announced in February 2026. Note: the BISP page still shows older rates of Rs 1,500 to Rs 4,000 — the rates above are the newer figures, and the disagreement is flagged here honestly.

What is the attendance rule for Taleemi Wazaif?

The child must attend at least 70% of school days. If attendance falls below 70%, the stipend can stop. Keep the child in school regularly and keep the school record updated.

My child's Taleemi Wazaif has not come. What should I do?

First check that the mother is still an active Kafaalat beneficiary and that the child is enrolled with 70% attendance. Then check the payment on the 8171 portal or by SMS. If it is still missing, visit the BISP Tehsil Office with the mother's original CNIC and the child's enrolment record.

Do I need to enrol my child separately for Taleemi Wazaif?

Yes. The child must be enrolled in school and registered for Taleemi Wazaif at the BISP Tehsil Office, with the mother's CNIC and the child's enrolment documents. Enrolment is free.
