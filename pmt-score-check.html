What Is the PMT Score?

PMT stands for Proxy Means Test. It is a score that measures how poor a household is, based on the information collected in the NSER (National Socio-Economic Registry) survey — things like household size, income, housing, and assets. A lower score means a poorer household. BISP uses the score to decide who is eligible for Kafaalat cash payments.

The score is set by the BISP Board. The current cutoff was set on 23 September 2021 and has not changed since.

PMT Cutoff Table — 32, and 37 for Disability

PMT scoreResultWhat it means
0 – 32EligibleHousehold qualifies for Kafaalat payments.
33 – 37Eligible only with a registered disabled memberQualifies only if the household has a registered disabled member (cutoff 37).
38 and aboveNot eligibleHousehold is above the cutoff and does not qualify.

How to Find Your Exact PMT Score

Your exact PMT score is not shown online — not on the 8171 portal, and not on any other website. The only way to find it:

  1. Go to your nearest BISP Tehsil Office. Ask for the NSER desk.
  2. Take your original CNIC. The desk checks your score against your CNIC.
  3. It is free. Do not pay anyone for this information.

If your household has a registered disabled member, tell the desk — the 37 cutoff applies only when the disability is registered.

Warning — "PMT calculator" scam: websites and apps claiming to calculate your PMT score from your CNIC or household details are scams. There is no public PMT calculator. The score is calculated by BISP from the NSER survey data only, and no website can show it. Never enter your CNIC on a PMT calculator site, and never pay anyone who promises to "fix" or "lower" your PMT score.

Above 32 — What to Do

If your score is above the cutoff, your household is not eligible under the current rules. Your options:

  • Check the score is right. Visit the Tehsil Office NSER desk and confirm your exact score and survey record.
  • If circumstances changed — for example, loss of income, or a death in the family — ask at the Tehsil Office about a re-survey. Note the rule: no re-survey is allowed within 2 years of your last survey.
  • Register a disabled member if one applies — the cutoff is 37 for households with a registered disabled member.
  • Do not pay agents. No agent can change your PMT score. Anyone asking for money to do so is a fraud — report them on helpline 0800-26477.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the PMT score for BISP?

The PMT (Proxy Means Test) score measures how poor a household is, based on the NSER survey. A lower score means a poorer household. BISP uses it to decide who is eligible for Kafaalat payments.

What is the PMT score cutoff for BISP in 2026?

The cutoff is 32. A household with a PMT score of 32 or below is eligible. For a household with a registered disabled member, the cutoff is 37.

How can I check my PMT score online by CNIC?

You cannot see your exact PMT score online. No website — not even the official 8171 portal — shows the exact number. The only way to find your exact score is to visit the BISP Tehsil Office NSER desk with your original CNIC. It is free.

My PMT score is above 32. What can I do?

If your score is above 32, your household is not eligible under the current cutoff. If your circumstances have changed — for example, loss of income or a death in the family — visit the Tehsil Office and ask about a re-survey. Note that a re-survey is not allowed within 2 years of your last survey.

Are PMT calculator websites real?

No. Websites claiming to calculate your PMT score from your CNIC or household details are scams. There is no public PMT calculator — the score is calculated by BISP from the NSER survey data only. Never enter your CNIC on a PMT calculator site.

What is the PMT cutoff for a household with a disabled member?

The cutoff is 37 for a household with a registered disabled member, instead of the usual 32. The disability must be registered — tell the survey team at the Tehsil Office and bring the disability certificate if you have one.
