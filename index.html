Check on the Official 8171 Portal

The check happens only on the official portal. It asks for exactly two things: your 13-digit CNIC number and the picture code (تصویر میں دیا گیا کوڈ) shown on the screen. No login, no password, no fee.

Open the Official 8171 Portal

The button opens 8171.bisp.gov.pk in a new tab. This is the official 8171 check online CNIC 8171 portal — the only address where the check happens. Enter your CNIC there — never on this site or any other site. If a page looks like the 8171 portal but has a different address, it is not official.

No internet? You can also check by SMS — send your CNIC to 8171.

8171 Check Online by CNIC — 4 Steps

  1. Open the official portal. Go to 8171.bisp.gov.pk. This is the only official portal address — check the address bar before entering anything.
  2. Enter your CNIC number. Type your 13-digit CNIC number, without dashes, in the first box. Use the CNIC of the woman of the household who is registered for Kafaalat.
  3. Enter the picture code. Type the characters shown in the picture (تصویر میں دیا گیا کوڈ) in the second box. If the code is hard to read, refresh it and try the new one.
  4. Press the check button and read your result. The portal shows one of five results. Find yours in the table below and follow the next step.

No account is needed at any step. If the portal does not open, try again later or use the SMS method. Full portal guide: the 8171 web portal, field by field.

What Your 8171 Result Means

The portal shows one of five results — this is the BISP 8171 result check online by CNIC. Here is what each one means and what to do next:

BISP Payment — Rs 14,500 (October–December 2026)

The current Kafaalat payment is Rs 14,500 per quarter for October–December 2026. Payments started 12 November 2026 and are released district by district, phase-wise, through six banks and wallets: HBL, HBL Microfinance, Bank Alfalah, Bank of Punjab, JazzCash, and Easypaisa. Wait for the SMS from 8171 before going to collect — it tells you the amount and the collection point.

Who Is Eligible for BISP Kafaalat?

Eligibility is decided by the NSER survey and the PMT (Proxy Means Test) score. The cutoff is 32 — a household with a score of 32 or below is eligible. For a household with a registered disabled member, the cutoff is 37. The beneficiary is the woman of the household. Your exact PMT score is not shown online — it is only available at the BISP Tehsil Office NSER desk, free, with your original CNIC.

Many people search for the 8171 check with an old year — 8171 check online 2023, 8171 check online 2022, or 8171 check online 2021. The 8171 check online CNIC Ehsaas Program check and the search "Ehsaas program 8171 online CNIC check" are the same BISP Kafaalat check — Ehsaas was the old programme name, and BISP runs it now.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I do the 8171 check online by CNIC in 2026?

Go to the official portal at 8171.bisp.gov.pk, enter your 13-digit CNIC number and the picture code shown on the screen, then press the check button. There is no login, no password, and no fee. This site is a guide only — it never asks for your CNIC and cannot check your eligibility for you.

Is there an 8171 login? How do I log in to 8171?

There is no 8171 login. The official portal has no account, no username, and no password — it asks only for your CNIC number and the picture code. Any website or app asking you to create an 8171 login is not the official portal.

What does 'Eligible' mean on the 8171 portal?

Eligible means your household passed the BISP survey and your PMT score is 32 or below (37 if the household has a registered disabled member). Wait for the payment SMS from 8171 telling you the amount and where to collect it. Do not pay anyone for the payment — collection is free.

What is the current BISP payment — Rs 14,500 or Rs 25,000?

The current payment is Rs 14,500 per quarter for October–December 2026, paid from 12 November 2026 through six banks and wallets. Rs 25,000 and Rs 13,500 are old amounts from earlier programmes and are not the current payment. A Rs 18,000 quarterly payment is planned from January 2027 but has not been notified yet.

Can I do the 8171 check online for 2023 or 2022?

Yes — the check works the same way whatever year you search for. Go to 8171.bisp.gov.pk and enter your CNIC and the picture code. The year in your search does not change the method or the result; the current payment is Rs 14,500 for October–December 2026.

How do I check my BISP result online by CNIC?

Enter your CNIC on the official 8171 portal (8171.bisp.gov.pk) with the picture code. The portal shows one of five results: Eligible, Not eligible, Under verification, Survey required, or No record found. Each result has a next step, explained in the table on this page.

What is the 8171 Rashan Program check online?

The Rashan programme is a different programme from BISP Kafaalat cash payments. This site covers the Kafaalat eligibility and payment check on the 8171 portal only. Do not confuse a Rashan message with a Kafaalat payment message.

How can I check my BISP payment by SMS?

Send your 13-digit CNIC number, without dashes, by SMS to 8171. You will get a reply about your eligibility or payment. If there is no reply, check the CNIC digits, wait a while and try again, or use the web portal instead. Full SMS steps are on the 8171 SMS check page.

What is the PMT score cutoff for BISP?

The PMT cutoff is 32 — a score of 32 or below makes a household eligible. For a household with a registered disabled member the cutoff is 37. Your exact PMT score is not shown online; it is only available at the BISP Tehsil Office NSER desk, free, with your original CNIC.

What is the BISP helpline number?

The BISP helpline is 0800-26477 (toll-free). You can also visit your nearest BISP Tehsil Office. The portal complaint tab was removed on 20 November 2026, so complaints now go through the helpline or the Tehsil Office.
