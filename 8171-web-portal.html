8171 Web Portal 2026 – Check BISP Payment by CNIC

Last verified: 24 Nov 2026

20 Nov 2026 change: the 8171 web portal now asks ONLY for your CNIC number and the picture code. The form number field, the mobile number field, and the complaint tab were removed. If a guide tells you to enter a form number or a mobile number, it is showing the old portal.

The Portal Form, Field by Field

Since 20 November 2026 the portal has exactly two fields:

  1. CNIC number — شناختی کارڈ نمبر. Type your 13-digit CNIC number, without dashes. Use the CNIC of the woman of the household who is registered for Kafaalat.
  2. Picture code — تصویر میں دیا گیا کوڈ. Type the characters shown in the picture on the screen, exactly as you see them. If the code is hard to read, refresh it and use the new one.
  3. Press the check button. Your result appears on the screen — Eligible, Not eligible, Under verification, Survey required, or No record found. See the result-meanings table on the homepage for what each result means and what to do next.

That is the whole form. There is no form number field, no mobile number field, and no complaint tab any more — all three were removed on 20 November 2026. If you see them on a guide or a screenshot, that guide is out of date.

There is no 8171 login. Many searches say "8171 check online CNIC login" or "8171 check online CNIC login 2026" — but no login exists. The portal never asks for a username or password. Any website, app, or agent asking you to create an 8171 login, or to log in to 8171, is not the official portal. Do not enter your CNIC or any password there.

Portal Not Opening? Captcha Not Working?

If the portal does not open or the picture code does not work, try these steps:

  • Check your internet connection and try again — a weak connection is the commonest cause.
  • Wait and try again later. The portal is slow when many people use it at once, especially after a new payment is announced.
  • Retype the picture code carefully. If it is hard to read, refresh the code and type the new one.
  • Try a different browser or a different phone if the page still does not load.
  • Use the SMS method instead. Send your 13-digit CNIC, without dashes, to 8171. See the 8171 SMS check guide.

If the portal keeps failing for days, visit your nearest BISP Tehsil Office with your original CNIC — they can check your record there.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 8171 web portal?

The 8171 web portal is BISP's official website at 8171.bisp.gov.pk, where you check your Kafaalat eligibility and payment by entering your CNIC number and the picture code. It is free and has no login.

How do I log in to the 8171 web portal?

You do not log in. The 8171 web portal has no login, no account, and no password. It asks only for your CNIC number and the picture code. Any page asking for an 8171 login is not the official portal.

What changed on the 8171 portal on 20 November 2026?

On 20 November 2026 the portal was changed to ask only for your CNIC number and the picture code. The form number field, the mobile number field, and the complaint tab were removed. Guides showing the old form are out of date.

Why is the 8171 portal not opening?

The portal can be slow or fail to open when many people use it at once, or when your internet connection is weak. Check your connection, wait a while and try again, or use the SMS method instead — send your 13-digit CNIC without dashes to 8171.

What is the picture code on the 8171 portal?

The picture code (تصویر میں دیا گیا کوڈ) is the set of characters shown in an image on the portal. Type exactly what you see in the image. If it is hard to read, refresh the code and try the new one.

How do I check my BISP payment of Rs 13,500 on the web portal?

Rs 13,500 is an old amount and is not the current payment. The current payment is Rs 14,500 per quarter for October–December 2026. Check it the same way on the official portal — CNIC number and picture code at 8171.bisp.gov.pk.
