BISP Registration & NSER Dynamic Survey 2026

Last verified: 24 Nov 2026

There is no online registration for BISP. Registration happens in person only, through the NSER dynamic survey at a BISP Tehsil Office or registration centre. It is free. Any website, app, or agent claiming to register you online — for a fee or for free — is fake.

To join BISP Kafaalat, your household must be surveyed. This page explains the survey, what to take, and what happens after.

How the NSER Dynamic Survey Works — 4 Steps

  1. Go to your nearest BISP Tehsil Office or registration centre with your original CNIC.
  2. Give your household details to the survey team. Answer honestly — the details decide your PMT score.
  3. Your PMT score is calculated. The cutoff is 32 (37 for a household with a registered disabled member). PMT score cutoff explained.
  4. Wait for the result SMS from 8171. If you are eligible, the SMS tells you about your payment. You can also check on the 8171 portal or by SMS to 8171.

The survey is free. Do not pay anyone for it, and do not pay anyone who promises to get you registered faster.

What to Take With You

  • Your original CNIC. A photocopy is not enough for the survey.
  • Any supporting documents the centre asks for about your household. The centre will tell you what is needed — do not buy documents from agents outside.

Re-Survey Rule — No Re-Survey Within 2 Years

If you have already been surveyed, no re-survey is allowed within 2 years of your last survey. If your circumstances have changed after that time — for example, loss of income or a death in the family — visit the Tehsil Office and ask about a re-survey.

Life Changes — Update NADRA First, Then BISP

If your family details have changed — a move, a marriage, a birth, or a death — update them in this order:

  1. Update your details with NADRA first and get your updated CNIC.
  2. Then go to the BISP Tehsil Office with the updated CNIC and get a token for the survey update.

Updating NADRA first matters — BISP reads your record from NADRA, so a change that is not in NADRA will not show in BISP either.

Next of Kin — If the Beneficiary Dies

BISP has a Next of Kin policy for the death of a beneficiary. The family should inform the BISP Tehsil Office with the death certificate and the beneficiary's CNIC, and the office will explain the Next of Kin process. Do not pay anyone for this — the process is handled by the Tehsil Office.

Old Registration Searches — 2022 and 2023

People still search for 8171 check online 2022 registration, 8171 check online 2022 registration date, and 8171 check online 2023 registration date. There is no registration date to look up — registration is the in-person survey, and it has no online date. The old search ehsaas program CNIC check online registration 2022 NADRA 8171 means the same thing: register through the NSER survey at the Tehsil Office, free.

Warning — registration scams: BISP registration is free and in person only. Anyone asking for money to register you, to do your survey, or to "confirm" your registration online is a fraud. Report them on helpline 0800-26477. How to report fake agents.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I register for BISP online in 2026?

There is no online registration for BISP. Registration happens in person only, through the NSER dynamic survey at a BISP Tehsil Office or registration centre. It is free. Any website claiming to register you for BISP online is fake.

What documents do I need for the BISP survey?

Take your original CNIC. The centre will tell you if anything else is needed for your household. Do not pay anyone for the survey — it is free.

What is the NSER dynamic survey?

The NSER dynamic survey is BISP's in-person household survey. The survey team records your household details and calculates your PMT score, which decides eligibility. The survey is free and happens at a BISP Tehsil Office or registration centre.

Can I do a re-survey if my first survey was wrong?

A re-survey is not allowed within 2 years of your last survey. If your circumstances have changed after that, visit the Tehsil Office and ask about a re-survey.

What happens to BISP payments if the beneficiary dies?

BISP has a Next of Kin policy for the death of a beneficiary. The family should inform the BISP Tehsil Office with the death certificate and the beneficiary's CNIC, and the office will explain the Next of Kin process.

I moved house / my family details changed. What should I do?

First update your details with NADRA, then go to the BISP Tehsil Office with your updated CNIC and get a token for the survey update. Do not pay anyone for this — the survey and updates are free.
