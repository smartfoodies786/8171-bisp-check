BISP Complaint & Helpline 0800-26477

Last verified: 24 Nov 2026

20 Nov 2026 change: the complaint tab on the 8171 portal was removed. You can no longer complain through the portal. Complaints now go through the helpline 0800-26477 or your nearest BISP Tehsil Office.

BISP Helpline — 0800-26477 (Toll-Free)

Call 0800-26477 for complaints, deductions, fake agents, and payment problems. It is toll-free. You can also visit your nearest BISP Tehsil Office in person. We do not publish helpline hours here, because sources disagree on them — the number itself is confirmed.

How to Complain — 2 Routes

  1. Call the helpline 0800-26477. Explain your problem and give your CNIC number to the helpline only. Note down any complaint or reference number they give you.
  2. Or visit the BISP Tehsil Office. Take your original CNIC and any receipt, SMS, or photo related to the problem. Ask for a receipt or diary number for your complaint.

What to Have Ready Before You Complain

  • Your CNIC number (the registered woman's CNIC).
  • What happened, when, and where — the date, the place, and the amount of money involved.
  • Your receipt from the collection point, if you have one.
  • Any SMS or photo related to the problem.
  • The name and number of the person involved, if you know them.

Do not share your CNIC with anyone except the official helpline or the Tehsil Office.

Reporting a Payment Deduction

If money was cut from your Rs 14,500 payment, report it at once:

  1. Count the full amount before leaving the collection point and ask for a receipt.
  2. Note the name of the person and the place where the deduction happened.
  3. Call 0800-26477 or go to the Tehsil Office and report the deduction with your receipt.

No one is allowed to take a cut from your BISP payment. Collection is free. Rs 14,500 payment guide.

Reporting a fake agent: if someone asks you for money to register you, to lower your PMT score, to release your payment, or to "confirm" your registration — they are a fraud. Do not pay. Report them on helpline 0800-26477 with their name and mobile number, what they asked for, and how much they demanded. Also report any SMS or WhatsApp message about BISP from a number other than 8171 — 8171 is BISP's only official number, and any other number is fake.

What You Can Complain About

  • Money deducted from your payment.
  • Payment not received although you are eligible and your district's payments have started.
  • Fake agents asking for money.
  • Fake SMS or WhatsApp messages about BISP.
  • Wrong survey record or PMT score — ask at the Tehsil Office NSER desk first, then complain if it is not fixed.
  • Taleemi Wazaif stipend missing or deducted.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the BISP helpline number?

The BISP helpline is 0800-26477 (toll-free). You can also visit your nearest BISP Tehsil Office. The portal complaint tab was removed on 20 November 2026, so complaints now go through the helpline or the Tehsil Office.

How do I complain about a BISP payment deduction?

Count your full payment before leaving the collection point and ask for a receipt. If any amount was deducted, report it on helpline 0800-26477 or at the BISP Tehsil Office. Keep your receipt and note the name of the person and place where the deduction happened.

Can I complain on the 8171 portal?

No. The complaint tab on the 8171 portal was removed on 20 November 2026. Complaints now go through the helpline 0800-26477 or the BISP Tehsil Office only.

How do I report a fake BISP agent?

Report fake agents on helpline 0800-26477 or at the Tehsil Office. Tell them the person's name and mobile number, what they asked for, and how much money they demanded. Never pay anyone who promises to register you, lower your PMT score, or release your payment.

What should I have ready before calling the BISP helpline?

Have your CNIC number ready, plus the details of your problem: what happened, when, where, and how much money is involved. Keep any receipt, SMS, or photo related to the problem. Do not share your CNIC with anyone except the official helpline or the Tehsil Office.

I got a BISP message from a number other than 8171. Is it real?

No. 8171 is BISP's only official number. Any SMS or WhatsApp message about BISP from another number is fake. Do not reply to it, do not click any link in it, and report the number on helpline 0800-26477.
