8171 SMS Check – Send CNIC to 8171

Last verified: 24 Nov 2026

No internet? You can check your BISP eligibility by SMS. Send your CNIC to 8171 and get a reply — no portal, no login, no app needed.

خلاصہ: انٹرنیٹ نہیں ہے تو ایس ایم ایس سے چیک کریں — اپنا 13 ہندسوں والا شناختی کارڈ نمبر بغیر ڈیش کے 8171 پر بھیجیں۔

How to Check by SMS — 3 Steps

  1. Open your phone's SMS app and start a new message to 8171.
  2. Type your 13-digit CNIC number, without dashes. For example: 3520212345678. Use the CNIC of the woman of the household who is registered for Kafaalat. Do not add your name, spaces, or dashes.
  3. Send it and wait for the reply. The reply tells you about your eligibility or payment — the same five results as the web portal.

Important: send only the CNIC of the registered woman of the household. Do not send other people's CNIC numbers, and never give your CNIC to an agent who offers to check it for you. The check is free to do yourself.

What the 8171 SMS Reply Means

The reply gives you the same result as the web portal. Here is what each reply means:

ReplyWhat it meansWhat to do next
EligibleYour household qualifies for Kafaalat payments.Wait for the payment SMS from 8171 with the amount and collection point.
Not eligibleYour household's PMT score is above the cutoff (32, or 37 with a registered disabled member).Check your exact PMT score at the Tehsil Office NSER desk.
Under verificationYour case is still being checked.Wait and check again after a few days.
Survey requiredNo survey record exists, or a new survey is needed.Visit the Tehsil Office or registration centre with your original CNIC for the NSER survey. It is free.
No record foundThe CNIC was not found in the BISP record.Check the CNIC digits and try again. If it still shows no record, visit the Tehsil Office to register.

No Reply or Delayed Reply — Fixes

If you sent your CNIC to 8171 and got no reply, work through these steps:

  • Check the CNIC digits. One wrong digit means no match. Retype the 13 digits carefully, with no dashes and no spaces.
  • Check you sent it to 8171. Sending to any other number will not work — and a reply from another number is fake.
  • Wait a while and try once more. Replies can be delayed when many people check at the same time, especially after a new payment is announced.
  • Check your mobile signal and balance. The SMS needs signal to send, and your network may charge for it (see below).
  • Use the web portal instead. If SMS keeps failing, check on 8171.bisp.gov.pk — CNIC number and picture code only. Web portal guide.
  • Still nothing? Visit your nearest BISP Tehsil Office with your original CNIC — they can check your record there.

Does the SMS to 8171 Cost Money?

Sources disagree on this, so we publish both: one source says the SMS to 8171 is free; another says Rs 2–3 applies without a package. The web portal check is free. Check with your mobile network for the exact SMS charge on your package.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my BISP eligibility by SMS to 8171?

Send your 13-digit CNIC number, without dashes, by SMS to 8171. You will get a reply about your eligibility or payment. Use the CNIC of the woman of the household who is registered for Kafaalat.

What does the 8171 SMS reply mean?

The reply tells you whether you are eligible, not eligible, under verification, or need a survey — the same five results as the web portal. If the reply says you are eligible, wait for the payment SMS from 8171 with the amount and collection point.

I sent my CNIC to 8171 but got no reply. What should I do?

First check the CNIC digits and that you sent it to 8171 with no dashes. Wait a while and try again once. If there is still no reply, check on the web portal at 8171.bisp.gov.pk, or visit your nearest BISP Tehsil Office with your original CNIC.

Does the SMS to 8171 cost money?

Sources disagree on this. One source says the SMS to 8171 is free; another says Rs 2–3 applies without a package. The web portal check is free. Check with your mobile network for the exact SMS charge.

Is the SMS check or the web portal better?

Both check the same BISP record. Use the web portal when you have internet — the result shows on screen at once. Use SMS when you have no internet or the portal is not opening.

Can I send someone else's CNIC to 8171?

Send only the CNIC of the woman of the household registered for Kafaalat. Do not send other people's CNIC numbers, and never share your CNIC with agents who offer to check it for you.
