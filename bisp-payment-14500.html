BISP Payment Rs 14,500 – October–December 2026 Qist

Last verified: 24 Nov 2026

Current qist: Rs 14,500 per quarter, October–December 2026. Payments started 12 November 2026 and are released district by district, phase-wise. Wait for the SMS from 8171 before going to collect.

The current BISP Kafaalat payment is Rs 14,500 per quarter for October–December 2026. This page explains where to collect it, what to take with you, and what to do if money is missing or deducted.

Open the Official 8171 Portal

Opens 8171.bisp.gov.pk in a new tab. Or send your 13-digit CNIC by SMS to 8171.

Where to Collect — Six Banks and Wallets

Payments are collected through these six banks and wallets:

  • HBL
  • HBL Microfinance
  • Bank Alfalah
  • Bank of Punjab
  • JazzCash
  • Easypaisa

Payments are released district by district, phase-wise — not every district at the same time. BISP publishes no exact district payment dates, so any post showing an exact date for your district is fake. Wait for the SMS from 8171, which tells you the amount and where to collect.

How to Collect Your Payment — 4 Steps

  1. Wait for the SMS from 8171. It tells you the amount and the collection point. Do not go before the SMS — your payment may not be ready yet.
  2. Take your original CNIC. Payment is given after biometric verification. A photocopy is not enough.
  3. Count the full amount before leaving. You should receive the full Rs 14,500. Ask for a receipt.
  4. Report any deduction at once. If anyone takes a cut from your payment, report it on helpline 0800-26477 or at the Tehsil Office. How to report deductions.

Old amounts — do not be confused: Rs 25,000 and Rs 13,500 are old amounts from earlier programmes. They are not the current payment. The current payment is Rs 14,500 for October–December 2026. If a message or post tells you to collect Rs 25,000 or Rs 13,500 now, it is out of date or fake. Searches for 8171 check online 25000, 8171 ehsaas program 25000 CNIC check online, benazir income support programme 8171 check online 25000, and 8171 check online CNIC ehsaas program 13500 all refer to these old amounts — not the current payment.

Rs 18,000 from January 2027 — Planned, Not Confirmed

A quarterly payment of Rs 18,000 from January 2027 has been announced by the government, but it has not been notified yet. Treat it as planned, not confirmed. The current confirmed payment remains Rs 14,500 for October–December 2026. This page will be updated when the January 2027 amount is confirmed.

Eligible but Payment Not Showing?

  • Your district may not have started yet. Payments are released phase-wise — wait for the SMS from 8171.
  • Check your status on the 8171 portal or by SMS to 8171.
  • Check the mother's CNIC — the payment is in the registered woman's name.
  • Still missing after your district started? Visit the BISP Tehsil Office with your original CNIC.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the current BISP payment amount?

The current payment is Rs 14,500 per quarter for October–December 2026. Payments started on 12 November 2026. Rs 25,000 and Rs 13,500 are old amounts and are not the current payment.

Where can I collect my BISP payment of Rs 14,500?

Payments are collected through six banks and wallets: HBL, HBL Microfinance, Bank Alfalah, Bank of Punjab, JazzCash, and Easypaisa. Payments are released district by district, phase-wise. Wait for the SMS from 8171 telling you where to collect.

What do I need to collect my BISP payment?

Take your original CNIC. Payment is given after biometric verification. Count the full amount before leaving, ask for a receipt, and report any deduction on helpline 0800-26477.

Is the BISP payment increasing to Rs 18,000 in January 2027?

A Rs 18,000 quarterly payment from January 2027 has been announced by the government, but it has not been notified yet. Treat it as planned, not confirmed. The current confirmed payment is Rs 14,500 for October–December 2026.

I am eligible but my BISP payment is not showing. What should I do?

Payments are released district by district, phase-wise — your district may not have started yet. Wait for the SMS from 8171. Check your status on the 8171 portal or by SMS. If your district's payments have started and yours is still missing, visit the BISP Tehsil Office with your original CNIC.

How do I check my BISP balance online by CNIC?

Check your payment on the official 8171 portal at 8171.bisp.gov.pk with your CNIC number and the picture code, or send your 13-digit CNIC by SMS to 8171. There is no separate balance-check website — the portal and SMS are the only official ways. This is the BISP 8171 online check balance method.
